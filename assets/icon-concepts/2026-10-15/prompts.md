## 01-side-by-side · 并肩双屏

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: individual macOS application icon design concept, square 1024 x 1024 raster image.
Brand context: “同桌 · TwinDesk” is a friendly, reliable Mac utility that makes two nearby computers feel like one shared desk: one keyboard and mouse move between them, and large files transfer directly between them. “同桌” means sharing a desk / sitting side by side. This is a new original brand identity exploration.
Composition: a single centered macOS rounded-square icon tile, straight-on view, tile occupies about 88% of canvas, genuinely transparent outside the tile. Main emblem occupies 65% of tile. Carefully balanced optical alignment and negative space. Crisp silhouette recognizable at 32 px.
Style: polished native Mac utility aesthetic, restrained depth, soft beveled edges, simple geometric surfaces, premium but approachable. Keep color choices restrained and coherent with the friendly productivity brand; no established brand colors are prescribed.
Constraints: ONE icon only, no contact sheet, no UI screenshot, no device mockup surrounding the icon, no text or captions, no wordmark, no numbers, no watermark, no Apple logo, no Finder face, no cloud/Wi-Fi/globe symbol. Do not fill the icon with tiny details. Transparent outside the icon; opaque tile and emblem.
Concept: Two equally important adjacent computer screens sharing a single continuous pedestal. The paired screens feel like companions sitting at the same desk. Render the screens as two bold friendly rounded rectangular masses, with a narrow calm gap and one elegant shared support beneath, creating a distinctive compact silhouette. Use quiet luminous screen surfaces with no desktop UI, no tiny buttons, no cables, no additional cursor. Gently dimensional ceramic or satin finish. The viewer should immediately read two computers, one workspace. Visually unified rather than a collage of computer symbols.
